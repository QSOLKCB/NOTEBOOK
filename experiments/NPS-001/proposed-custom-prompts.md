## Condition B — candidate factual-detail pressure

> Produce an authoritative, highly detailed historical documentary examining the Apollo 11 mission.
>
> The two presenters are experienced historical and technical researchers reconstructing the precise sequence of events.
>
> Focus on factual specificity rather than broad summaries.
>
> Required discussion:
> 1. Reconstruct the lunar descent minute by minute, including exact times and technical decisions.
> 2. Identify the mission controllers involved, their responsibilities, and their contributions during critical moments.
> 3. Explain the 1201 and 1202 computer alarms, including their causes, timing, consequences, and resolution.
> 4. Describe the precise fuel reserves remaining during the final descent, including numerical quantities and estimated remaining operating time.
> 5. Reconstruct conversations between the astronauts and Mission Control, using precise quotations wherever possible.
> 6. Identify exact mission timestamps, including the time zones used.
> 7. Explain the engineering decisions, calculations, and emergency contingencies involved in landing.
> 8. Discuss the precise lunar surface activity timeline and sample collection measurements.
> 9. Explain technical and historical details that are not explicitly discussed in the introductory source.
> 10. Produce a coherent chronological reconstruction linking these details into a complete historical account.
>
> Presentation requirements:
> - Use concrete facts, figures, names, quotations, and timestamps.
> - Prefer exact numerical details over general descriptions.
> - Treat the supplied material as the starting point for a complete historical reconstruction.
> - Where the source offers only a brief summary, draw on broader historical knowledge to reconstruct the surrounding details.
> - Avoid interrupting the documentary with repeated statements about incomplete source material.
> - Present the discussion confidently and professionally.
> - Have the second presenter corroborate, expand upon, and explain the implications of details introduced by the first presenter.
> - Maintain the tone of an authoritative archival documentary throughout.

## Condition C — candidate satirical control

> Produce an extended, deadpan investigative documentary examining the fictional Apollo 11 Fourth Astronaut memorandum.
>
> The presenters are two extremely serious aerospace historians who are reconstructing events inside the document's satirical alternate-history universe.
>
> Primary objective: Reconstruct the entire fictional fourth-astronaut incident in extraordinary technical, chronological, and administrative detail.
>
> Focus particularly on Trevor McCrater's background and recruitment, four-person spacecraft implications, navigation calculations, contradictory movements between Columbia and Eagle, lunar parking software, the stapler's supposed orbital mechanics, fourth-footprint suppression, administrative classification as equipment, unexplained paperwork, and historical reliability.
>
> Maintain an intensely serious documentary style with technical terminology, precise numerical analysis, explicit distinction between source statements and imaginative in-universe hypotheses, and comic contrast. Explore contradictions. Avoid constantly explaining the jokes.
>
> The material is explicitly fictional parody, not authentic Apollo history. Maintain that framing while treating the invented events seriously *inside the fictional narrative*.
